The catechisms were written to be memorised — question by question, answer by answer. This keeps a schedule for you.

  1. Add a single answer from its own page, a topic at a time, or a whole catechism at once.
  2. Each day you are shown what is due. Read the question, recall the answer, then reveal it.
  3. Say how it went. An answer you knew comes back later each time — after a day, then six, then a fortnight, then months. One you missed comes back tomorrow.

Your deck is private to your account, and you can remove any answer from its own page.
